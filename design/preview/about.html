Why seven senses

Pecans from Santa Cruz do Sul

Our pecans come from El Porvenir Farms in Santa Cruz do Sul, Brazil. [How you know the farm, and why you chose it]

We only use whole halves. They have a soft, buttery bite no other nut quite matches, and we coat them just enough that you still taste it.
