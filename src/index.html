    Origin · southern Brazil

    Four real seasons

    1. Spring · September to November

      Pecan trees wake up late. Long green catkins hang from the branches and the wind does the pollinating.

    2. Summer · December to February

      Hot days in Rio Grande do Sul. The nuts grow inside green husks, out of sight.

    3. Autumn · the harvest

      The husks split and the pecans drop. Harvest runs through the southern autumn. [VERIFY exact harvest months with El Porvenir]

    4. Winter · June to August

      Bare branches and real cold. Pecans need that chill to rest, which is why they grow here, in the south of Brazil, and not in the tropics.

    5. 10 687 km from our farm

      El Porvenir, our own farm in Santa Cruz do Sul, is 10 687 km from the Netherlands. It grows part of what we sell; the rest comes from partner farms that work the same way. [TBD which farms and where]
